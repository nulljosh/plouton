Brand kit and design system

Talli

Talli keeps a tally of your benefits: what lands, when, and what you can still earn. Every colour on this page is read live from the tokens the site uses.

The mark

Four strokes and a slash: a tally. One bold white glyph on a blue gradient, made in Icon Composer (Talli.icon). No text, no outline. Clear space equals one stroke. Smallest size 16 px.

Art

Public domain and CC0 engravings and sculpture, dithered to halftone dots and tinted on the blue band. Glyphs sit in scanlines. Every image is used once on the site.

Colour

Shown for your current light or dark mode. Blue is the only colour Talli owns. #5B9BD5 is the brand blue; #2F6FB0 is the same blue deep enough for text and white-label fills on light (5.2:1).
